## Key Points

### Hackathon Format
- **Partner**: Cerebral Valley
- **Participants**: 500 selected builders
- **Credits**: $500 API credits per participant
- **Timeline**: One week to build
- **Prize pool**: $100,000 in Claude API credits across 6 winners
- **Notable**: 4 out of 5 winners were not professional developers

### First Place: CrossBeam (Mike Brown)
- **Builder**: Personal injury lawyer, not a developer
- **Problem**: California housing permits have 90%+ rejection rate, 6-month delays costing $30k
- **Solution**: AI parses blueprints and correction letters, assigns targeted agents to each correction, generates action plan in 20 minutes
- **Two-sided**: Helps both builders (fix permits) and municipalities (batch-process submissions)
- **Pilot user**: Buena Park, CA (needs 8,900 housing units by 2029, permitted only 120 in 2024)
- **Quote**: "I didn't write a single line of code. I didn't even read a line of code."
- **GitHub**: github.com/mikeOnBreeze/cc-crossbeam

### Second Place: Elisa (Jon McBee)
- **Builder**: Software engineer, built for 12-year-old daughter's science fair
- **Solution**: Block-based visual IDE where users snap together primitives (goals, requirements, agents, skills, rules)
- **Stats**: 30 hours, 76 commits, 39,000+ lines of code, 1,500+ tests
- **Use case**: Daughter programmed microcontroller without writing code
- **Vision**: Software creators stop writing source code, work with tests and specifications
- **Prize**: $30,000 API credits for classroom funding
- **GitHub**: github.com/zoidbergclawd/elisa

### Third Place: PostVisit.ai (Michał Nedoszytko)
- **Builder**: Brussels-based cardiologist, 20 years building healthcare software
- **Solution**: Post-appointment suite explaining diagnoses, analyzing visit notes and AI-scribe transcripts
- **Features**: Surfaces clinical evidence with physician oversight, combines health records + evidence + visit data
- **Development**: Built during road trip from Brussels to San Francisco
- **Previous work**: Previsit.AI (AI patient intake in Belgium, Greece, Poland)
- **Website**: postvisit.ai

### "Keep Thinking" Prize: TARA (Kyeyune Kazibwe)
- **Builder**: Uganda Ministry of Works and Transport (at time of hackathon)
- **Problem**: Traditional feasibility studies cost $1-4M, take 9-14 months, ignore community impact
- **Solution**: Dashcam footage → complete investment appraisal using Opus 4.6 vision
- **Analysis**: Surface conditions, roadside activity, auto-populates costs, NPV, cash flow, equity score
- **Real example**: Kira-Matugga Road (Uganda) - complete PDF report in 5 hours vs weeks
- **GitHub**: github.com/Kye256/tara-transport-assessment

### Special Prize — Creative Exploration: Conductr (Asep Bagja Priandana)
- **Solution**: Browser-based MIDI instrument, Claude as live virtual bandmate
- **Features**: Real-time generation of drums, bass, melody, harmony as you play
- **Innovation**: Natural language control ("make it funky", "build to a climax") mid-jam
- **Technical**: C engine compiled to WebAssembly, generates notes every 15ms
- **Stats**: ~4,800 lines of JavaScript and WebAssembly
- **GitHub**: github.com/nanassound/conductr
