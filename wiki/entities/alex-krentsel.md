## Overview

Alex Krentsel is a PhD student at UC Berkeley's NetSys Lab, advised by Scott Shenker and Sylvia Ratnasamy, with additional collaboration in the Sky Lab with Ion Stoica. Primarily focused on networking and control systems, Krentsel gained recognition for his deep-dive analysis of OpenClaw's architecture and the design principles underlying autonomous agent systems.
