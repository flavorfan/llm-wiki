# Eric Seu

## Overview

Eric Seu is a thought leader on AI applications in marketing, particularly known for predicting the dramatic scaling of marketing experimentation through [[concepts/auto-research]].

## Characteristics

**Key insight:**
- Recognized the transformative potential of AutoResearch for marketing teams
- Articulates the shift from low-volume to high-volume experimentation
- Focuses on quantifying the pace of change (30 experiments/year → 36,000/year)

**Notable quote:**
> "Most marketing teams run 30 experiments per year. The next generation will run 36,000"

This represents a 1,200x increase in experimentation throughput - approximately 100 experiments per day.

## Common Strategies

**Marketing experimentation vision:**
- Apply [[concepts/auto-research]] to marketing workflows
- Automate A/B testing at massive scale
- Test emails, ad creatives, landing pages, headlines, thumbnails, YouTube titles
- Use AI agents to modify copy and measure conversions
- Follow same loop as code optimization: modify → test → keep or discard
